# Standby Diesel Generator Automated Test & Fuel Polishing SOP

Source: KB-SRC-017 · Owner: Power Team

## Emergency genset operational readiness
1) Automatic weekly no-load test runs (15 minutes, every Tuesday 10:00 UTC) to
circulate lubricants and test starter batteries. 2) Monthly on-load transfer test
via the ATS (30 minutes under site load) to prevent engine wet stacking. 3)
Diesel fuel quality testing every 6 months: measure water content (under 200ppm)
and bacterial sediment. 4) Run the mobile fuel polishing unit if microbial
contamination or algae bloom is detected in the bulk storage tank. 5) Maintain
the fuel day tank at a minimum 85% capacity (equivalent to 18 hours of continuous
full-load run time).

## Mobile genset dispatch
A mobile genset dispatched to a site in outage takes approximately 45-90 minutes
to arrive from depot. Dispatch it as soon as estimated battery backup time falls
under 4 hours; do not wait for the battery to actually run out.
